Annual Leaves – Contractual Associates
The following leave policy will be implemented for those Associates working as Contractuals:
Associates are entitled to 12 working days of annual leave (January-December). This is inclusive of casual and sick leaves. Leaves accrue at 1 day per month. This will be pro-rated for Associates joining Centegy during the middle of the month/year.
Associates are encouraged to avail annual leaves.
No leaves will be allowed to carry over/accrue to the next year. Unavailed leave balance will lapse at the end of the year. Any leaves taken in excess of how much is earned during the year will be deducted from salary.
Associates must submit the Leave Request Form online and have it approved by their Supervisor.
Leaves will not be encashed, at any time.
Effective 1 January 2026, associates who have completed two years of continuous service, subject to recommendation and approval by management, will be entitled to 24 annual leaves per year. This entitlement is inclusive of casual and sick leaves. Leaves will accrue at 2 days per month. For associates who complete two years of service during the year, the entitlement will be pro-rated from the date of completion of their two-year tenure.
Annual Leaves
Associates are entitled to 24 working days of annual leave (January-December). This is inclusive of all leave types. Leaves accrue at 2 days per month (of working time). This will be pro-rated for Associates joining Centegy during the middle of the year.
Associates are encouraged to avail annual leaves.
At year-end a maximum of 5 days leave is allowed to be carried over/accrued to the next year. Any leave balance more than 5 will lapse. Any leaves taken in excess of how much is earned during the year will be deducted from salary.
Associates must submit the Leave Request Form online and have it approved by their Supervisor.
There is no leave encashment policy except at the time of final settlement. This policy applies to confirmed Associates and not probationary Associates.
Half-day Leave
Associates are given the benefit of half-day leave. This leave can be availed during your working hours. Associate must work at least 4 hours consecutively in a day in order for it to be considered a half-day. All timing and attendance policies will be applicable.
Two half days will be treated as one full day of leave.
An online Leave Request Form must be submitted and approved by the Supervisor.
Hajj Leaves
Associates, with minimum one year service, are entitled to Hajj leave of up to 40 calendar days. This leave will be granted only once during the entire period of service, and requires management approval. A declaration/Leave Request must be submitted, stating that this is the first Hajj being performed during employment at Centegy. Any subsequent leaves taken on behalf of Hajj will be deducted from annual leaves.

Maternity Leaves
Female Associates are entitled to maternity leave for 3 months (90 calendar days) with pay. This leave can be taken as per mutual consent, either before or after delivery or a combination of both. Paid maternity leave may be availed only twice in an Associate’s service with the company.
Negative Leave Balance
Associates are expected to avail their leave entitlement within the current calendar year in alignment with their supervisor. It may be useful if a leave calendar is prepared at the beginning of the year to distribute team leaves throughout the year.
All Associates must submit a Leave Request Form (LRF) in case of negative leave balance.
A medical certificate must be submitted if a leave due to sickness is taken for more than 2 days.
Effective 1st September 2026, a sandwich leave policy will apply to Associates who take additional leave after exhausting all their allocated leaves for the year. A sandwich leave occurs when a weekend or public holiday falls between two leave days. In such cases, the intervening weekend or public holiday will also be deducted from the employee’s leave balance as leave availed.
Leaves during Ramzan
Associates are not entitled to take a half-day leave during Ramzan, but the half-day penalty remains effective.

Sum of 6 months basic salary
Total PF balance

In case of Haj, maximum entitlement is the LOWER of (a), (b), or PKR 25,000/-
Repayment: in a maximum of 48 equal installments.
Interest-free loan
Purpose for which the loan is requested and who is eligible:
Two PF loans cannot be taken simultaneously
After deduction of monthly loan installment, a minimum of 50% take-home salary must remain in member’s possession
*as per Income Tax law and regulations
**family member refers to spouse, children, parents, and dependent siblings
***evidence of vehicle purchase must be submitted
Procedure:
Loan request form can be downloaded from Centegy Desk.PF Members are required to fill out the Member Details and PF Loan Request Details sections. A current month pay slip must be attached to the form

Member will submit the completed form to HR Department along with respective Departmental Head approval, pay slip, PF statement and necessary document/s as evidence
HR Department will review the request and obtain approvals from PF Trustees
Upon loan approval, Finance Department will complete the request form, make arrangements for loan disbursement and subsequently create PF loan in Payroll. Loan deductions will commence in the month after loan disbursement PF Loan Request Form

FAQs
FAQs
I was at a client since morning and have arrived late. Shall I check-in on the time machine?
Yes, you always need to check-in on the time machine, regardless of the time you arrive. Since you arrived late due to client visit, you can make an adjustment using the adjustment mechanism.
I have to be at a client and have to make an early departure. Shall I check-out on the time machine?
Yes, you always need to check-out on the time machine, regardless of the time you leave. Since you have an early departure due to a client visit, you can make an adjustment using the adjustment mechanism.
Can I adjust hours worked during extra days (weekends and public holidays) against any deficient hours during the week (Mon-Fri)?
No, you cannot adjust your weekly deficient hours with your extra work days. For extra work days, you are compensated otherwise. For further clarification refer to Centegy Desk-Company Policies- Meal/Conveyance claims.
Are standard working hours and deficient hours checked for the month (1st to 30th /31st) or for the fortnight (16th to 15th)?
Standard working hours and deficient hours are checked for the last completed month. E.g. In May, working hours of April will be evaluated.
Do I have to submit a Leave Request Form (LRF) for taking a single leave?
Yes, a Leave Request Form must be submitted for each leave day availed. If there are multiple single days in the month, they can be incorporated on one LRF.
Do I have to submit a Leave Request Form (LRF) for availing half-day leave?
Yes, a LRF must be submitted for availing half-day leave.
How to record machine time during planned late night activities?
Associates checking-in at or after 4:00 pm are required to press F1(a) to change the time mode(c) from check-out to check-in of the time machine before they clock-in
Associates checking-out at midnight or after midnight are required to press F2(b) to change the time mode(c) of the time machine before they clock-out
It is mandatory to check-in/check-out using time machine; do not pass manual adjustments during planned late-night activities
Kindly ensure that the machine is on the correct time mode when you check-in/check-out
The entire time should be recorded on the same day when the activity started and not on the next day
Key:
Time Mode: Check-in / Check-out on time machine
Example Scenario:
If an Associate check-in after 6:00 pm on Friday evening, Associate should press F1 to change the time mode of machine before check-in. Similarly, when an Associate leaves office late at night, for e.g. 12:30 am, Associate should press F2 before they check-out to record the time correctly.

Employee Feedback Survey
The Employee Feedback Survey is to obtain necessary employee feedback with regards to three main areas: work culture, leadership, and information security. By providing such a platform, it demonstrates the company’s commitment to achieving employee satisfaction which in turn plays a crucial role in creating a positive and productive work environment.

The survey can be filled out by all employees at any time of the year.
Procedure:
The Employee Feedback Survey will be available on Centegy Desk. It is available to all Associates and can be filled out at any time during the year
Surveys will be analyzed on a bi-annual basis and survey results will be shared with Management and relevant Department Heads
Action plans may be developed and implemented to address key findings and suggestions
Progress will be tracked and reported to Management
Anonymous Feedback & Suggestions
For Associates wanting to provide feedback anonymously, they may do through the Suggestion Boxes placed in Room 603 and at the entrance of 501. Both these areas are secure and accessible to all Associates.
Procedure:
Feedback and suggestions may be given handwritten or typed and they can be of any nature keeping within professional boundaries
Every two months, the suggestions will be retrieved and compiled
Necessary action plans will be developed and shared with Management for further implementation
Enabling Associates to provide their feedback and suggestions encourages open communication and transparency throughout the organization. All Associates are encouraged to participate.
