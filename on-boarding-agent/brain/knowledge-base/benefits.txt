Health Insurance:
Centegy Technologies provides medical insurance coverage for employees,
with options to add dependents. Coverage starts on the first day of the
month following your start date. Plan options and enrollment are handled
through the benefits portal during your first week.

Retirement / Provident Fund:
Employees are enrolled in a company provident fund with employer
contributions matched according to local regulations at their office
location (Dubai, Karachi, or Lahore). Enrollment is handled by HR during
onboarding.

Other Perks:
- Annual learning & development budget for courses, certifications, or
  conferences relevant to your role
- Company-provided laptop and equipment for your role
- Team social events and gatherings

Requesting Details:
Ask HR for the full benefits package specific to your office location, as
offerings vary by country.
