Centegy Technologies -- Company Overview

Centegy Technologies is a technology solutions provider that licenses,
implements, and supports enterprise applications for the Sales &
Distribution and Insurance industries. The company's motto is "People.
Expertise. Integrity."

What we do:
- Sales & Distribution: our flagship S&D Pro suite (the Sales &
  Distribution Management Suite) covers secondary sales, distributor
  operations, sales force automation, outlet and customer management, and
  asset management.
- Insurance Solutions: Java-based policy and claims administration
  platforms for general and health insurers, including ILAS, the General
  Takaful Suite, and GLAS.

Scale:
- Sales & Distribution: 30 global implementations, around 50,000 system
  users, and around 3,000,000 outlets served.
- Insurance: 44 solution implementations, around 1,000,000 policies under
  administration, around 400,000 claims processed, and around
  200,000,000 in gross written premiums/contributions.
- Centegy's solutions are implemented across roughly 66 countries in the
  Middle East, Africa, Asia, and the Pacific.

Notable clients include Unilever, L'Oreal, Shell, Colgate, Alinma Tokio
Marine, LIA, Oriental, Jubilee Life, Adamjee Insurance, ABF Sugar, and
Takaful Limited (UK).
