Paid Time Off (PTO):
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of PTO per year, credited monthly
(1.67 days per month). PTO can be used for vacation, personal time, or any
reason -- you don't need to specify why when requesting it. Unused PTO up
to 5 days can be carried over into the next calendar year; anything beyond
that is forfeited unless local law requires otherwise.

Sick Leave:
In addition to PTO, employees get 10 paid sick days per year for illness,
medical appointments, or caring for an immediate family member. Sick days
do not carry over and are not paid out on termination.

Holidays:
Centegy Technologies observes paid public holidays according to local
labor law at each office location (Dubai, Karachi, and Lahore), plus a
company-wide end-of-year closure.

Parental Leave:
Employees who have been with the company for at least 6 months are
eligible for paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or
fostering of a child, in line with local labor law at their office
location.

Requesting Time Off:
Submit requests through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance
for planned absences. Sick leave can be reported the same day. Your
manager approves PTO requests; HR should be looped in for any leave
longer than 2 consecutive weeks.
