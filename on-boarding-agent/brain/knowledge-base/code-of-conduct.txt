Respect and Professionalism:
Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment,
discrimination, or retaliation of any kind is not tolerated and should be
reported to your manager or HR immediately -- you can also report
anonymously through the ethics hotline linked in the HR portal.

Working Hours:
Standard office hours apply at each location (Dubai, Karachi, Lahore),
with flexibility coordinated through your manager. Client-facing teams
should plan around overlap with client time zones when needed.

Confidentiality:
Customer data, source code, and internal business information are
confidential. Don't share credentials, discuss unreleased features
publicly, or use company data outside of work systems.

Conflicts of Interest:
Disclose any outside employment, consulting, or financial interest that
could conflict with your role at Centegy Technologies to your manager and
HR.

Expenses:
Business expenses should be submitted through the expense portal within
30 days, with receipts attached. Ask your manager if you're unsure
whether something is reimbursable before purchasing.

Dress Code:
Business casual for the office; client-facing meetings and demos may
warrant more formal attire.
